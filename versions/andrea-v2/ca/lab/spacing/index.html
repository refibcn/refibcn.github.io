Containers & gutter

--gutter (2rem) is the horizontal page padding almost every section uses. Two content widths:

  • --container-default — min(1280px, 100% − 2×gutter), most sections
  • --container-wide — min(1440px, 100% − 2×gutter), hero + map-heavy layouts
  • --container-prose — 65ch, long-form text measure
live

Radius

--radius and --radius-sm are both 0 (flat, BIS-straight), defined in src/styles/theme.css. --radius-pill and --radius-full are universal (tokens.css), used for badges and avatars.

--radius · --radius-sm · --radius-pill · --radius-full

live

Shadow

All three steps collapse to the same flat 1px shadow, on purpose — elevation reads through borders and translate-on-hover, not drop shadow, in the flat BIS direction.

live

Motion

--t-fast (120ms), --t-med (280ms), --t-slow (600ms, custom ease) — all collapse to 0ms under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, so hover/reveal transitions degrade to instant rather than being disabled ad hoc per component.
