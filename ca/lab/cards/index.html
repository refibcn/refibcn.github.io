Cards

Four card-shaped molecules, all built on the .card primitive (global.css §6.3) but diverging in props and — in two cases — never actually used. Body copy in every card runs on .card__text (var(--copy-md), 17px) — the M tier of the copy scale, see Typography — one step down from the page's regular reading copy.

unused on real pages

ProjectCard vs. ServiceCard

Nearly identical: title + body + link on .card. ProjectCard is used by NetworkSection (the Regenerant Catalunya cohort pages). ServiceCard has zero usages anywhere — what-we-do.astro doesn't render it, despite the name suggesting that's where it belongs. Candidate to merge into one Card with an external prop, once it's clear whether what-we-do actually needs the extra field ProjectCard has.
