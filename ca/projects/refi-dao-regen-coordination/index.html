ReFi DAO / Regen Coordination

Role
Local node · governance design · funding-round coordination
Theme
Network coordination
Where
Global federation

ReFi BCN is a local node within the wider ReFi DAO and Regen Coordination network. We contribute governance design, funding-round mechanics, and the day-to-day coordination that keeps a distributed regenerative ecosystem moving.

In practice this means bridging two worlds: the cooperative and Economia Social i Solidària traditions we are rooted in locally, and the global regenerative-finance infrastructure — Safe, Karma GAP, Gitcoin, Celo Public Goods — that the network runs on. We name those tools specifically and use them where they add value, without letting the tooling drive the work.
